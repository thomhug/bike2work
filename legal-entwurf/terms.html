What this is

velo.tom.li is a private, non-commercial website about cycling and bike commuting, run by a single individual. It publishes ride data, analyses and videos from the operator's own cycling.

The software behind it

A set of private scripts collects the operator's own data from services the operator uses — Strava, Garmin, Withings, YouTube, Instagram and TikTok — and turns it into statistics and video descriptions. The software is used by the operator alone. It is not offered to other users, there is no sign-up, and no accounts other than the operator's own are accessed.

Use of the site

The content is provided as is, for information only. Training figures, power values and physiological estimates describe one person's data and are not advice. Anyone acting on them does so at their own risk.
