Privacy Policy

velo.tom.li — last updated 28 September 2026

Who is responsible

This site and the software behind it are run by a private individual in Switzerland for personal, non-commercial use. Contact details are on velo.tom.li.

What data is accessed

The software accesses only the operator's own accounts, using access tokens the operator has granted to their own applications:

  • TikTok — the operator's own profile name and the list of the operator's own videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.
  • YouTube and Instagram — equivalent data for the operator's own channel and account, plus comments left on the operator's own posts.
  • Strava, Garmin, Withings — the operator's own ride and body-weight measurements.

No data of other users is collected. There is no sign-up, no login for visitors, and no way for anyone else to connect an account.

Comments by other people

Comments left publicly on the operator's own posts are read so that replies can be written. They are not stored beyond what the platform already shows publicly, are not analysed for advertising, and are not passed on.

Where the data is stored

Locally, on the operator's own computers, in plain files. Access tokens are stored with file-level access restrictions and are never published. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

The site sets no tracking cookies and uses no advertising or analytics services of third parties.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the settings of the respective platform. Local copies are deleted on request.

Contact

Questions about this policy: via the contact details on velo.tom.li.
